# A one-minute walkthrough

This is an illustrative demo script, not a real family story or a guarantee of the exact response.

1. Open the Replit project and start its preview if needed.
2. Enter: “My daughter slammed her door, and I am thinking of taking her phone away.”
3. Explore the companion's question and clearly labeled fictional scenarios.
4. Try “I need a next step now” or the wording control. Edit the takeaway to fit the situation.
5. Switch to the teen experience: draft what you wish a parent understood, skip a prompt, and inspect the editable preview. Keep identifying details out.

The goal to demonstrate: a parent gets room to think, and a teen gets room to express a perspective. A calmer conversation remains a goal, not a measured result.
