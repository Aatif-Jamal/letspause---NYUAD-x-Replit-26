# Privacy and demo boundaries

- Leave names, schools, locations and other identifying details out of stories.
- Teen stories are stored as pending submissions. A deletion receipt is provided; keep it if you want to remove your submission.
- Moderation remains locked, and teen submissions are not used by the companion.
- Parent takeaways can optionally be saved on the device. Clear controls and session expiry exist in the prototype.
- Do not assume absolute anonymity, zero tracking, or production-grade security; hosting and operational handling have not been audited here.
- Scenario stories are fictional. No real family submissions or private conversations belong in this public repository.
- The PS5 raffle is inactive. Sharing does not enter a giveaway.

Before public use, review moderation, retention, safeguarding and the actual data flows. These notes describe the prototype, not a compliance certification.
